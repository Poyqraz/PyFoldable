# Contributing to PyFoldable

Issues and review feedback are welcome. A code, documentation, configuration, or data
pull request must:

1. identify its source and any third-party material;
2. include tests or evidence appropriate to the change;
3. preserve fail-closed validation and provenance contracts; and
4. include this exact statement in the pull-request description:

   > I have read and agree to the PyFoldable CLA.

The [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) lets contributors keep their copyright
while granting the Project the rights needed to maintain both the public
noncommercial distribution and separate commercial licensing. A contribution is not
accepted merely because it was submitted; maintainers must verify the statement
before merge.
